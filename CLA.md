Everyone sending code to `write` signs this once. A bot comments on your first
pull request with a link; signing is a single click and applies to every later
contribution.

## Why this exists

`write` is GPL-3.0-or-later and stays that way. This agreement does not change
your license to use the app.

What it does is keep two options open for the project:

- **Dual licensing.** Offering a paid commercial license alongside the GPL to
  organisations that cannot ship GPL code, the way Qt and MySQL have. It
  changes nothing for individual users and funds the work.
- **Releasing the `.docx` engine separately** under a permissive license
  (Apache-2.0 or MIT), so other projects can use it.

Both require the project to hold the rights to all of the code. Without a CLA,
one merged pull request removes both options permanently, because a
contribution can only be relicensed by the person who wrote it.

You keep the copyright in your own work. This is a license, not an assignment.
